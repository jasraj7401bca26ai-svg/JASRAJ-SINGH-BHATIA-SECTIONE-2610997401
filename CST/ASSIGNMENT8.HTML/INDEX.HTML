Culture and Traditions

India is famous for its unity in diversity. People celebrate festivals such as Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas and Baisakhi with happiness and enthusiasm.

Indian culture includes traditional music, dance, colourful clothing, art, languages and delicious regional cuisines. It teaches us the values of respect, love and togetherness.
