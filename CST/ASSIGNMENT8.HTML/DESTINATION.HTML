Top 5 Tourist Destinations

  1. Delhi – Famous for its historical monuments.
  2. Goa – Popular for beautiful beaches.
  3. Manali – Known for mountains and snowy landscapes.
  4. Jaipur – Famous for royal forts and palaces.
  5. Agra – Home to the magnificent Taj Mahal.

Five Things to Carry While Travelling

  • Water bottle
  • Comfortable clothes
  • Identity card
  • First-aid kit
  • Mobile phone and charger

Famous Places in Delhi

  • India Gate – A famous war memorial.
  • Red Fort – A historic Mughal monument.
  • Qutub Minar – A remarkable historical tower.

Explore Beautiful Destinations

1. Delhi – The Heart of India

Explore the history, culture and famous landmarks of Delhi.

2. Goa – Paradise of Beaches

Enjoy golden beaches, beautiful sunsets and relaxing views.

3. Manali – Beauty of the Mountains

Experience mountain scenery, fresh air and peaceful nature.

4. Jaipur – The Pink City

Jaipur is known for its royal heritage, colourful markets, beautiful palaces and magnificent forts.

5. Agra – City of the Taj Mahal

Agra attracts visitors from around the world with the world-famous Taj Mahal and its rich history.

Travel Tips

  • Plan your journey in advance.
  • Keep your important documents safe.
  • Respect local culture and traditions.
  • Keep the places you visit clean.
  • Capture memories and enjoy your journey!
