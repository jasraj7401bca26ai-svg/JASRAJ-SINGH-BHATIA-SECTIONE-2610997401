📖 5. College Terminology

Here are five important terms related to college life and education.

No. Term Meaning
1 Semester An academic period into which a college year is divided.
2 Lecture A teaching session in which a teacher explains a topic.
3 Assignment A task given to students to practise or demonstrate learning.
4 Attendance A record of a student's presence in classes and lectures.
5 Examination A test used to evaluate knowledge and understanding.
